## First steps
<!-- tags: begin, sign in, entrar -->

Sign in, then open **Manual** in the navbar. Every chapter keeps the language you chose; a chapter that has no
translation yet is shown in English with a notice.

![The demo dashboard](images/dashboard.svg "Click the figure to enlarge it")

## Moving around

Use the chapter list on the left, the pager at the bottom, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> in the browser.
